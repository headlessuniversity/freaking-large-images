A complete space adventure

Our comprehensive week-long program combines education, excitement, and hands-on experience to inspire the next generation of space explorers.

Rocket building workshops.
Design, build, and launch your own model rockets under expert guidance. Watch them soar and learn the physics of flight.
Astronaut training simulations.
Experience what real astronauts go through with our zero-gravity simulator, mission control exercises, and spacewalk training.
Planetarium shows.
Journey through the cosmos in our state-of-the-art planetarium with guided tours of constellations and distant galaxies.
Expert instructors.
Learn from aerospace engineers, astronomers, and former NASA employees who bring real-world space experience to every lesson.
Mars rover programming.
Code and control real robotic rovers, learning programming fundamentals while exploring simulated Martian terrain.
Team missions.
Collaborate on space station repair missions and lunar landing challenges that build teamwork and problem-solving skills.

Frequently asked questions

Our Stars welcomes young explorers ages 8-16. We organize activities and groups by age to ensure everyone has an age-appropriate and engaging experience. Younger campers (8-11) and older campers (12-16) participate in different mission levels.

We offer weekly sessions throughout the summer from June 1st through August 31st. Each session runs Monday through Friday, with residential campers checking in Sunday evening and departing Saturday morning. You can register for multiple weeks if your child wants an extended adventure!

Not at all! Our programs are designed for all knowledge levels, from complete beginners to budding astronomers. Our expert instructors adapt activities to meet each camper where they are and help them grow their understanding throughout the week.

The camp fee covers all activities, materials, instruction, meals, snacks, and a camp t-shirt. Residential campers also receive lodging and evening programs. The only thing your child needs to bring is their enthusiasm for space exploration!

Safety is our top priority. All staff are background-checked and trained in first aid and CPR. We maintain small group sizes with a 1:8 instructor-to-camper ratio. Our facilities are regularly inspected, and we follow strict safety protocols for all rocket launches and activities. Medical staff are on-site at all times.
